About

I am an Integrated MS/PhD student in Computer Science at the Theory of Computation (ToC) Lab, Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Yo-Sub Han. My research focuses on enhancing the reliability and efficiency of programs with large language models (LLMs). On the efficiency side, I go beyond predicting time complexity to generating inputs that expose real algorithmic bottlenecks, so that inefficiency can be verified through execution. On the reliability side, I study whether generated code satisfies not only its required functionality but also its input contracts, building benchmarks that quantify this and generation methods that raise specification satisfaction. Since neither property can be judged from the final output alone, I take a neuro-symbolic approach: LLMs interpret natural language, while structuring and verification are delegated to deterministic rules, constraint solvers, and actual execution.

Research Interests

  • Efficiency: predicting time complexity and generating inputs that expose algorithmic bottlenecks
  • Reliability: benchmarking and generating code that satisfies both functionality and input contracts
  • Testing: specification-driven test generation with LLMs, verified by execution
  • Method: neuro-symbolic pipelines that combine LLMs with rules, constraint solvers, and execution
