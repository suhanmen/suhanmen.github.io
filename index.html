About

I am an Integrated MS/PhD student in Computer Science at the Theory of Computation (ToC) Lab, Yonsei University. My research focuses on program analysis and code intelligence, particularly time complexity prediction, test case generation, and multi-agent debate frameworks. I explore how large language models (LLMs) can reason about algorithmic properties and generate specification-aware test suites, integrating chain-of-thought prompting, reinforcement learning, and self-play mechanisms to improve the reliability and coverage of generated test cases.
